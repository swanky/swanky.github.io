## Design Context

### Users
Four overlapping audiences visit this site:
1. **Photography clients** — individuals or brands considering hiring Swanky for portrait/commercial shoots
2. **Tech/corporate partners** — companies seeking blockchain consulting, agile coaching, or technical leadership
3. **Photography fans and followers** — people who follow Swanky's award-winning work (PX3, IPA, TIME Magazine)
4. **Media and press** — journalists, editors, publishers looking for bio, coverage, and contact info

All four audiences need to quickly understand: *who is this person, why are they credible, and how do I engage?*

### Brand Personality
**親切 × 多才多藝** — Approachable and Versatile.

Swanky is a rare professional who spans two very different worlds: award-winning portrait photographer (TIME Magazine, PX3 France, IPA USA) and senior tech leader (Taiwan Mobile manager, PhD from NTNU, blockchain researcher, agile coach). The brand should feel like that rare person who is genuinely excellent at things that don't usually go together — warm and human, not cold and corporate; accomplished but not arrogant.

Three-word personality: **Genuine · Multifaceted · Distinguished**

### Aesthetic Direction
**Mixed creative professional** — spans art + tech, playful yet authoritative. Not purely a photographer's portfolio (minimalist, image-forward only) nor a tech consultant's site (dry, corporate). The site should feel like an editorial magazine — visual, curated, human — but with enough gravitas to signal serious professional credentials.

- **Theme**: Light mode, editorial warmth. The gold (#E5A300) is a strong brand asset — lean into it as a signature, not just a button color.
- **Typography**: Should feel editorial and distinctive — not system defaults. Current Open Sans + Roboto is forgettable.
- **Photography**: The award-winning photography work should be the hero of the site. Currently it's buried. Images should breathe and dominate.
- **NOT**: Cold blue tech startup, dark mode with glowing accents, generic Bootstrap template feel, AI-generated homogeneity.

### Design Principles
1. **Lead with craft** — The photography is TIME Magazine level. The site should feel worthy of that work. Images first, credentials follow.
2. **One identity, many facets** — Rather than splitting "photographer" and "tech guy" into separate silos, the design should present one coherent, multi-dimensional person.
3. **Gold as signature** — #E5A300 is the clearest brand differentiator. Use it with intention: as accent, highlight, and identity marker — not slathered everywhere.
4. **Editorial rhythm** — Vary section treatments dramatically (full-bleed image, text-heavy, minimal, dense) to create a magazine-like reading experience rather than a series of identical rows.
5. **Chinese-first confidence** — The site is Traditional Chinese (zh-TW). Lean into this fully. Mixed Chinese/English calls-to-action ("Read More" on a Chinese page) should be resolved. The typography and layout should be optimized for Chinese text rendering.
